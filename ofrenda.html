In loving memory

Rosa Delgado

1931 – 2019

my grandmother

For Rosa Delgado, who always kept the kettle on

She was always the first to welcome you, a warm “Come in, there’s coffee” before you even stepped inside. I remember her mornings, rising before dawn to light the ovens with my grandfather, a quiet dedication to her little bakery. It was a simple life, full of routine and love.

I can still picture her, in the photo at the sewing machine, carefully selecting buttons from that old biscuit tin, letting us choose our Halloween costumes. She loved boleros on the radio, humming along while she cleaned, a peaceful rhythm to her days. We’ll put some pan de muerto, café de olla, and a few of her favourite orange marigolds on the ofrenda for her today.
