In loving memory

Rosa Delgado

1931 – 2019

my grandmother

For Rosa Delgado, who always kept the kettle on

We’ve set out a little of everything she loved. The pan de muerto is for her, of course, and a pot of café de olla to always be ready. We remember her saying, “Come in, there’s coffee,” and we want to offer that warmth to her now. The orange marigolds, bright and cheerful, just like she was.

In the photo at the sewing machine, you can see her, focused and happy, letting us choose the buttons from her biscuit tin. She spent so many Saturday afternoons listening to boleros on the radio, humming along while she cleaned. It’s a quiet comfort to remember that.

We’ve put out a tin of buttons, just like she always did, and we hope she hears us, enjoying the music and the simple pleasures she cherished.
